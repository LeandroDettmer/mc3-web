Boots past the Rockstar logos and the intro movie, none of them played. MC3_SKIP_MOVIES=hard

    Midnight Club 3: DUB Edition Remix in the browser: the Xbox game's own code, statically recompiled to C with xboxrecomp and built for WebAssembly, drawn with WebGPU (WebGL 2 as a fallback). Plays on PC, phones (touch controls) and TVs, with keyboard or controller.

    Online: free roam with other players in the same city, and the garage with every car and part unlocked. Your online profile is kept in this browser.

    You need your own copy of the game: extract your disc and serve it from your own machine with tools/web_serve.py or web/server. Nothing of the game ships with this page; saves stay in this browser.

    Source: github.com/LeandroDettmer/mc3-web. Bugs and ideas: open an issue there; contributions by fork and pull request.

    mcdub3 web by Leandro Dettmer, a browser port of mcdub3-nx by Laure Ladastra (MIT).

    The city is online

    Midnight Club 3 like you never played it: real players, real races, every ride in the garage.

    • Online Free Roam

      The city never sleeps, and now it's never empty. Cruise with real players, name tags over their cars, every one a blip on your map.

    • Online Career

      Your career, someone else's taillights. Real players fill your career city, and your save stays yours alone.

    • Street Races

      Pull up next to rivals, flash the invite, hit Accept. Up to 8 racers on the start grid, a countdown, then it's just you and the asphalt.

    • Race From the Map

      Every start point in the city is live. Roll up, press White, and the AI is already on the line. Bring your friends along.

    • Everything Unlocked

      Every city, every car, every part. $2,000,000 in your pocket the moment you go online. Go spend it.

    • Drift for Cash

      Hang the tail out, chain the drifts, stack the multiplier. Online wins and drift points pay real prizes and unlock more.

    • Ghost Rivals

      In AI and career races on a PC, the players nearby race on as ghost cars. Someone's always on your bumper.

    • Play Anywhere

      PC, phone with touch controls, or the TV. Keyboard or controller. Up to 144 fps if your screen keeps up.

    Saved in this browser. Changes apply the next time you press Play. “Default” leaves the game's own choice.

    Passed to the game:

    The keyboard is pad 1 (Settings, Keyboard to turn it off). Click the game first so it gets the keys.

      Xbox, PlayStation and other controllers work through the browser's Gamepad API, as pads 1 to 4 in the order they are connected; one plugged in later is picked up within a second. Press a button on it once the game is running so the browser shows it to the page.

      Only this controller drives pad 1 here, so two tabs can each use their own. Remembered for this tab only. The keyboard acts on the focused tab.

      The keyboard stays on top of pad 1. Buttons sit where the Xbox pad has them: A, B, X, Y, Start, Back, the triggers, White and Black on the shoulders, both sticks and their clicks.

      Profiles in this browser

        Saved in this browser's private storage (OPFS), under UDATA/54540079/; the game keeps the last two copies of each one and puts the newest good one back if a save is ever cut short. Export all saves downloads every profile, the online profile and those copies as one .zip: keep it somewhere safe. Download saves a profile (the Remix's is .mc3Remix, the DUB Edition's .mc3). Import takes one downloaded from this site (another browser or device) or, for the DUB Edition, from an Xbox, and signs it for the port; a file of the other game is refused; memory-unit images (.gsx) come later, until then make import-save FILE=…. PS2 profiles can't be used.

        Snapshots

          Every 2 days, when it starts, the game copies all your saves (profiles and the online profile) into a dated snapshot in this browser; snapshots over 7 days old are removed, the newest always kept. Restore puts a snapshot's saves back (before Play) and keeps the current ones as a snapshot first.

          Play together

          Everyone who plays on this site shares one online world. Nothing to set up: it is always on.

          In the game, Online is the first item of the main menu; the number beside it is how many players are in Free Roam now.

          Free Roam: pick Online to drive the city with everyone who picked the same city. Every car and part is unlocked.

          Online Races: hold White (Q or Shift on the keyboard, a shoulder button on a gamepad) while you pick Online, then Online Races: Host Game to start a race, or Join Game to race in someone else's.

          Set Your name below: it is what the other players see.

          What other players will see. Shown in the game's sign-in texts and on the server link.

          Online profile

          Free Roam's money and garage, apart from your career, in this browser's private storage (online/profile.bin). Saved when you leave the garage and when you go back to Career, Tokyo or Arcade. An import counts from the next Free Roam.

          Races

          Make a race or join one here, before or while the game runs. Then in the game: Online, Online Races, and Host or Join Game: only the players of your race show there. Not in a race: you meet everyone else not in one, as before.
